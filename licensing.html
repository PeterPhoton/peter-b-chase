The Work

Photographs made on the ground,
not at a desk.

Six landscape and travel series spanning the Sierra Nevada high country, Patagonia, the American Southwest desert, the Mediterranean, and beyond — selected here for their commercial installation appeal.

Every image is made in the field by one person with a camera. The resulting photographs have a specificity — of light, of place, of moment — that rewards long acquaintance. They are, in that sense, well-suited to permanent installation.

No AI. No stock. No generation.

Every image in this portfolio was made by a human being standing somewhere on the earth, looking at something real. The provenance is unambiguous: camera, photographer, location, moment.

In a market increasingly saturated with generated imagery, that distinction is no longer subtle — it is the point.

The work moves between black and white and color, between landscape and documentary, between the monumental and the intimate. Prints are produced to archival standards on request. Licensing terms are negotiated directly, per project.

Process

How it works.

Every licensing relationship starts with a conversation.

Tell me about the space, the intended use, and the number of images you're looking for. I'll review the available work and put together a selection suited to the project. Licensing terms — including exclusivity, geographic scope, and duration — are negotiated directly, without an intermediary.

For large installations or multi-property agreements, I'm available for consultations with design teams and art directors. For editorial use, turnaround is typically within 48 hours.

All inquiries are handled personally. No automated responses, no assistants.

Licensing fees

Single-use editorial licenses begin at $500. Single-image commercial and hospitality installations typically range from $1,500 to $5,000, depending on use, territory, and duration. Multi-image and multi-property agreements are priced by scope. Exclusive rights are available at a premium.

Every transaction is covered by a simple one-page license agreement. Delivery is digital — high-resolution file via secure transfer, within five business days of payment.
